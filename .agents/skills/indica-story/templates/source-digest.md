## 8. The debate and its history
- **Live debate:** who argues what now, the evidence each side leans on, and what

- **History that explains the present:** the earlier rounds that set today's terms,
  each with a source. Leave this empty if nothing earns its place.
- **Sources still to find:** facts the story needs that are not yet sourced. These are
  not usable until they are sourced.
